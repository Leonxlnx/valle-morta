# Valle Morta

A first-person zombie shooter set in a war-ruined alpine valley, running in the browser on three.js (WebGL2).

Every visual asset is generated by code in this repository: the eroded mountain range, the valley, the ruined village with its church and enterable houses, the broken bridge, the river, grass and trees, the tank wreck, the three weapons with their animated arms, the four kinds of infected and all PBR texture sets. No premade models or textures are used. Sound effects and music are licensed third-party recordings (see Credits).

## Run it

```bash
npm install
```

```bash
npm run dev
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:5173. A GPU with WebGL2 is required; the game is tuned on an integrated Radeon 780M and has four quality presets in Settings.

## Modes

- **Survival**: hold the village against wave after wave. Waves mix walkers, runner packs and brute escorts, a boss comes every fifth wave, and each cleared wave leaves a supply crate with ammunition and bandages. You start in the village street.
- **Operation**: a run-based mode with permanent progress. A helicopter drops you at a landing zone (the meadow south of the river or the village square), waves escalate without end, and kills and cleared waves earn salvage. Extract by helicopter to bank it all; death ends the run and banks part of it. Between runs the **Base** screen spends salvage on weapon upgrades (damage, magazine, reload, recoil, sniper unlock, double shot, armour-piercing and incendiary rounds), body upgrades (armour, health, sprint, healing, bandages, softer landings), abilities and run modifiers. Progress is saved in the browser (`localStorage`).

## Weapons

| Weapon | Fire | Magazine | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| AK-pattern rifle, 7.62 | automatic, 600 rpm | 30 | damage falls off beyond 60 m |
| 9 mm pistol | semi-automatic | 15 | fast draw, unlimited reserve |
| Bolt-action sniper rifle | bolt, 1.1 s cycle | 5 | real scope view, bipod when prone, passes through up to three bodies |

## Abilities (Operation)

Bought at the Base and fitted to the G, 4 and 5 keys, each with a cooldown ring on the HUD: a parachute-dropped tank you drive for about 12 seconds, a mortar barrage, a sentry gun, adrenaline (a few seconds of slow motion) and a jump pack.

## Controls

| Action | Input |
|---|---|
| Move / look | W A S D / mouse |
| Fire / aim | left / right mouse button |
| Reload | R |
| Next weapon / direct | Q / 1 2 3 |
| Sprint / jump | Shift / Space |
| Crouch / prone | Ctrl or C / Z (toggle) |
| Dodge roll | V |
| Use bandage | H |
| Interact | F |
| Ability (Operation mode) | G, and 4 5 for further slots |
| Pause | Esc |

Health comes in four segments and regenerates only to the top of the current one; bandages (up to three) do the rest. Falls above 3.5 m hurt; a roll on landing halves the damage.

## How it is built

- `src/engine`: HDR renderer with ray-marched volumetric clouds baked into a sky panorama, cloud shadows, light shafts, height fog, ambient occlusion and quality presets.
- `src/world`: terrain clipmap and far mountains, procedural buildings (seven enterable houses with stairs, a debris ramp and the inn's balcony), the bridge, grass, trees, water and props.
- `src/game`: player controller, weapons and view models, infected with flow-field navigation and per-bone hit volumes, the wave director, Operation mode with its abilities and saved progress, effects and audio.
- `src/ui`: menus, settings, the Base screen and the HUD with its aerial minimap.
- `src/cinematic` and `cinematic/`: a scripted film rendered from the live game.
- `docs/DESIGN.md`: the rules and contracts the game is built to.

## Tools

All tools drive the real game in headless Chrome against the running dev server, take a GPU lock (`tools/lib/gpulock.mjs`) so only one runs at a time, and write to `tools/_preview/`.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `node tools/playtest.mjs` | the full automated play-test (`--only game,ui,operation`, `--sec move,nav,...`) |
| `node tools/housewalk.mjs` | walks through every doorway, stair, ramp and balcony of the enterable houses; checks infected reach a player indoors and that the spawn points are clear |
| `node tools/vmshots.mjs` | contact sheets of each weapon: hip, walk, sprint, aim, fire, reload, scope, prone |
| `node tools/uishots.mjs --w 1280 --h 720` | menus, settings, Base and the HUD in both modes |
| `node tools/zslip.mjs` | planted-foot slip of each infected type and gait |
| `node tools/perf.mjs`, `node tools/shot.mjs --bench` | frame timing |
| `node tools/bake-terrain.mjs` | re-bakes the erosion-simulated terrain |
| `node cinematic/capture.mjs --stills` | quick framing check of the film (without `--stills` it renders the full 4K HDR film, which needs ffmpeg with libx265 and takes a long time) |

Offline generators live in `tools/texgen/` (PBR textures, Python + numpy) and `tools/zombie/`, `tools/weapon/`, `tools/props/` (Blender scripts).

## Known limitations

- The look is a real-time approximation of the photographic references it was built from, not a match for them.
- The weapons, arms and infected are fully procedural and show it at close range. Aiming the rifle, the receiver fills the lower part of the view.
- The middle span of the bridge is blown out: the bridge cannot be crossed on top; the river can be waded.
- Infected planted feet still slide by roughly 10 % of their travel while they steer round corners.
- The wave bell is a temple bell recording, because no freely licensed church bell could be found; nobody has auditioned the sound set by ear.
