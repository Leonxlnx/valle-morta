# Valle Morta — design and contracts (iteration 2)

This is the shared reference for everyone working on the game. Read the sections that concern your task; the contracts (names, units, files) are binding.

## Rules that hold everywhere

- No premade 3D assets or textures. Geometry, textures, rigs and animation are generated by code (JS or Blender scripts in `tools/`).
- Sounds are licensed recordings from online sources only (CC0 or CC BY, recorded in `public/assets/audio/CREDITS.md`). Never synthesise a sound.
- Everything must be physically plausible: nothing floats or intersects, weapons handle like real firearms, bodies have weight.
- Quality and performance come first. Target: 60 fps or better at 1280x720 on the "high" preset on an integrated Radeon 780M (about 14 ms per frame in `tools/shot.mjs --bench`), no hitches.
- UI: clean, modern, minimal and clearly designed, not a default template. One sans-serif family (Inter). Neutral palette with at most one restrained accent, a muted red only for danger. No serif, no monospace, no all-caps or letter-spaced eyebrow labels, no clutter.
- The machine is a laptop shared with other jobs and must never be overloaded: one GPU job at a time (`tools/lib/gpulock.mjs`, already used by `shot.mjs` and `playtest.mjs`), Blender with at most 6 threads and CPU Cycles at low sample counts, check `powershell -NoProfile -File tools\sysmon.ps1` before long jobs.
- The vite dev server already runs at http://127.0.0.1:5173. Never start another. `?auto=1` is the tooling mode (no menu, `window.__vm` API).
- Only edit the files your task owns. Do not commit; the lead commits.

## Player

- Field of view setting 60 to 110 degrees.
- Fall damage: none below a 3.5 m drop, rising to lethal at about 14 m. A roll within 0.3 s of landing halves it.
- Dodge roll: about 0.6 s and 4.5 m in the movement direction, 1.2 s cooldown, the camera dips and tilts (no full camera flip).
- Prone: eye height about 0.35 m, crawl 0.9 m/s, much steadier aim. The sniper's bipod deploys when prone and still.
- Health 100 in four segments. It regenerates only up to the top of the current 25 point segment. Bandages (carry up to 3, +35 HP, 1.6 s to apply) come from supply crates and occasional drops.
- Damage feedback: directional arcs at the screen edge, short camera kick, muffled audio at low health.

### View-model asset contract

One file per weapon: `public/assets/models/rifle.glb`, `pistol.glb`, `sniper.glb`. Metres, +Y up, barrel along -Z, +X to the shooter's right, origin at the pistol grip.

- Node `Weapon`: parent of all weapon parts. Moving parts are separate named nodes: rifle `Bolt`, `Magazine`, `Trigger`, `Selector`; pistol `Slide`, `Magazine`, `Trigger`, `Hammer`; sniper `BoltHandle`, `Magazine`, `Trigger`, `BipodL`, `BipodR`, `Scope`.
- Node `Arms`: an armature with a skinned mesh `ArmsMesh` (both forearms, gloves, real fingers with three bones each). Arms reach back out of frame.
- Empties: `Muzzle`, `Eject`, `SightRear`, `SightFront` (line parallel to the barrel), sniper `ScopeEye` (centre of the ocular, looking along -Z). A small mesh `Casing` (spent case, hidden template).
- Animation clips with exactly these names, authored in Blender and baked at 60 fps, animating the arm bones and the weapon part nodes together: `Idle` (loop), `Fire` (one shot, parts and trigger finger), `Reload` (magazine change with rounds left), `ReloadEmpty` (ends with chambering), `Draw`, `Holster`, `Sprint` (loop). Sniper also `BoltCycle` and `BipodDeploy`. Pistol `Fire` moves the slide.
- Clip lengths: rifle `Reload` 2.4 s, `ReloadEmpty` 3.0 s; pistol 1.7 s and 2.1 s; sniper `Reload` 3.2 s, `BoltCycle` 1.1 s; `Draw` 0.45 s (pistol 0.3 s); `Holster` 0.3 s.
- Sidecar `public/assets/models/<id>.anim.json`: `{ "clips": { "Reload": { "duration": 2.4, "events": [ { "t": 0.5, "name": "mag_out" }, { "t": 1.5, "name": "mag_in" }, { "t": 1.5, "name": "ammo" } ] } } }`. Event names: `mag_out`, `mag_in`, `bolt_back`, `bolt_forward`, `slide_release`, `ammo` (magazine is full from here), `shell` (eject a case).
- Budgets: weapon up to 60k triangles, arms up to 20k, 2048 textures, file under 12 MB.

The engine plays the clips with an AnimationMixer and layers sway, bob, aim offset and recoil on the root. It must keep working with a model that has no clips.

## Infected

| type | look | speed | health | behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `walker` | existing villager | 0.9 to 1.3 m/s | 100 | shambles, lunges |
| `runner` | lean, hunched | 4.5 to 5.5 m/s | 60 | sprints in packs, flanks |
| `brute` | 2.1 m, heavy | 1.6 m/s | 450 | body shots do half damage, hard knock-back hit |
| `boss` | 2.5 m hulking figure | 2.5 m/s, charges at 7 m/s | 2600 | ground slam, appears every fifth wave, health bar on the HUD |

Bone names and the base clip names stay as in `src/game/zombies.js`. Extra clips: `Sprint` (runner), `Roar` and `Slam` (boss). Locomotion must look natural: weight shift, arm swing, head lag, no foot sliding (the engine scales playback to ground speed).

Blood: impact puffs, decals on the ground and on walls behind a hit, a pool that grows under a corpse.

## Modes

1. **Survival**: the wave mode. Waves mix types, some waves are special (runner pack, brute escort), a boss every fifth wave, supply crates with ammunition and bandages between waves. The player starts inside the village.
2. **Operation**: a run-based mode with permanent progress. A helicopter drops the player at a landing zone, waves escalate without end, kills and cleared waves earn salvage. Death ends the run and returns to **Base**, a designed screen where salvage buys permanent upgrades. Progress is stored in `localStorage`.
   - Weapons: per weapon damage, magazine size, reload speed, recoil control (five levels each); unlock the sniper; perks such as double shot, armour-piercing and incendiary rounds.
   - Body: armour, maximum health, sprint speed, healing speed, bandage capacity, softer landings.
   - Abilities with cooldown timers on the HUD: deployable tank (drive it for about 12 s and run the infected over), mortar barrage, sentry gun, adrenaline (short slow motion), jump pack (a strong boosted leap).
   - Run modifiers: salvage multiplier, start at a later wave.

## HUD

Minimap top left, drawn from a top-down render of the real terrain so it reads like an aerial photograph, with the player arrow, nearby infected as soft markers, the boss, crates and objectives. Health segments with bandage count, ammunition with the weapon silhouette, ability cooldown rings, wave and threat, damage direction arcs, hit and kill markers.
