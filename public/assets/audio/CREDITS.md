# Audio credits

All audio is third-party recorded material, redistributed under the licences below. Nothing was synthesised; processing was limited to ffmpeg trimming, fades, gain normalisation (peak about -1 dBFS) and MP3 conversion. Where a piece is CC BY 4.0 (Kevin MacLeod), attribution is required: "Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0".

Notes: surface and object labels are best-effort and in places only approximate. step_grass is a hiking boot on grass; step_wood is shoes on a wooden floor. shell_drop_1-3 are brass casings dropped on concrete (used as stone), shell_drop_4-5 come from a recording of casings of various sizes bouncing on an unspecified hard surface; no dirt recording was found. impact_flesh uses wet slap recordings (no gunshot-into-flesh recording with a suitable licence was found); blood_splat uses a blood-splat clip, a spit clip and a slime squish. roll and land_hard are cut from a foley recording of a body hitting the ground (the third land_hard variation is a heart-to-ground thud); no gear rattle is included. mag_out, mag_in are an AK-47 reload; bolt_rack is a charging-handle stroke of an RPK (AK-pattern) rifle; pistol_mag_in is cut from the handgun reload recording. bolt_back/bolt_forward are a Mosin Nagant bolt (lift and pull, push and lock). sniper_shot is a Tikka T3 in .30-06 bolt action (.308 was not available in the library). explosion_1-2 are generic explosion recordings and explosion_3 is a cannon hit on a ship from the Battle at sea pack, none is a recorded mortar impact; mortar_whistle is cut from falling-bomb recordings; boss_slam_1 is a large creature landing and boss_slam_2 a cannon hit on a wall; sentry_fire is a recorded machine-gun burst; tank_tracks is a tracked-vehicle recording; heli_loop is a helicopter hover recording and heli_distant a second helicopter recording at the same level (use a low volume). zombie_run, brute_growl and boss_roar are creature/troll/monster recordings chosen by name and length and are only approximately what the logical name suggests. ui_buy, ui_upgrade, ui_error and ability_ready are cut from metal clink/bing/thud/bong recordings; ui_hover is a recorded swish; pickup is a latch click. music_base is the first 184 s of Floating Cities (full track) with an 8 s self-crossfade. Attribution required (CC BY 3.0): roll_1-3 and land_hard_1-3 contain material from the Super Foley Pack by dklon (OpenGameArt). Source timestamps refer to the original file; freesound sources are the site's preview mp3.
